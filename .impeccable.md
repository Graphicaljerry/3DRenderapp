# Impeccable — project design context

Captured 21 Aug 2026 from Jerry's own statements across the build sessions, not inferred
from the codebase. Update it when he corrects something.

## Design Context

### Users

**The app (Moldable):** desktop 3D-printer owners who need a part that must fit a physical
object already in their hand — a bracket, an adapter, a replacement clip. They own calipers.
They already hold an LLM API key (the app is bring-your-own-key, which filters the audience
before anyone sees the product). They are at a desk or a workbench, often mid-project, and
they arrive after a downloaded model came out 2 mm wrong. Secondary: small job shops making
jigs; prop and cosplay makers, for the engineering around a sculpt, never the sculpt.

**Documents and decks (audits, playbooks, reports):** an audience of one — Jerry, a
designer-developer who knows HTML and CSS and some JavaScript, and does not live in build
tooling, LLM plumbing or CAD kernels. He reads these at a desk to decide what to build next.
He wants outcomes and honest caveats, not process.

### Brand personality

**Measured. Workshop-honest. Unhurried.**

The app's whole claim is that a number is right. Everything should carry that: state what is
true, show the measurement, name the caveat out loud. Never oversell, never hedge into mush.
Tone is a good technical manual — plain, confident, specific. Sixth-grade reading level where
the subject allows.

### Aesthetic direction

- **Anti-reference, in Jerry's own words:** AI slop. The repo carries `skills/no-ai-slop/SKILL.md`
  and it is binding — no purple-blue gradient heroes, no emoji as icons, no identical card
  grids, no decorative sparkles, no "Elevate / Seamless / Unleash" copy.
- **The app's own contract** (from that skill): Schibsted Grotesk display, system body,
  JetBrains Mono for machine values. Product UI keeps that contract.
- **Documents may have their own identity** and should not simply reskin the app. They are
  reading surfaces: paper-like, editorial, generous measure, real typographic hierarchy.
- **Theme by context**, not by habit: the app is a workshop tool used on a dark canvas and
  ships light and dark; long-form documents default to light, because they are read.
- Vary the type and palette between documents. Do not converge on one house look.

### Design principles

1. **The number is the point.** Wherever a figure exists, show it, and show what it was
   measured against. Tabular figures, aligned columns, no rounding that hides an error.
2. **Say the caveat where the claim is**, not in a footnote. An honest limit stated next to a
   strong claim is what makes the strong claim credible.
3. **Structure must encode something true.** Numbers mean a real sequence; severity labels
   mean a real ranking. No decorative numbering, no ornamental rules.
4. **Left-aligned, asymmetric, generous.** Reading matters more than impact. Cap the measure,
   vary the spacing, never centre a page of prose.
5. **Earn every element.** If a border, card, icon or animation is not carrying information,
   delete it.
