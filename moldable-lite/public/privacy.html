Privacy

Last updated: 21 August 2026

Moldable is a local-first app. The short version: your designs, chats, photos and API keys live in your own browser, and the app works without an account.

What stays on your device

  • API keys you paste into Settings are stored in your browser's local storage on your device. They are sent only to the AI provider they belong to (or through the relay to that provider, unread and unstored). They are never synced to our servers.
  • Projects, chat history, photos and models are stored in your browser (localStorage and IndexedDB) unless you sign in and sync.

What leaves your device

  • Your prompts and attached images are sent to the AI provider you configured (for example Anthropic, Google, OpenRouter, a mesh-generation service) so it can generate your model. Their handling of that data is governed by their own privacy policies. You choose the provider; we don't see this traffic.
  • If you sign in to sync, your projects (designs, chat transcripts, small thumbnails — not your API keys, not full-resolution photos) are stored with our database provider (Supabase) under your account, so your other devices can load them. Your email address is used to operate your account.
  • The optional relay (a small proxy that some generation providers require in a browser) forwards your requests to the provider. It does not store your keys or your content.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising, no sale of data.
  • We don't train AI models on your designs.
  • We can't read the API keys you use — they never reach a server we keep data on.

Deleting your data

Signed out: clearing this site's data in your browser removes everything. Signed in: deleting projects in the app deletes them from sync too; to close your account entirely, contact us and we'll remove it.
